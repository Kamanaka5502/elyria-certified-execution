## Contribution posture

This repository is **not open source** and is **not accepting unsolicited contributions**.

Do not submit pull requests, patches, derivative implementations, certification engines, production adapters, deployment connectors, runtime-enforcement modifications, customer-report logic, or protected certification methodology unless you have written authorization from Samantha Revita and Terry Snyder / Elyria Systems — VA.

## Permitted public feedback

Public feedback may be limited to:

```text
documentation clarity
public certification-level clarity
public proof-requirement clarity
unsafe interpretation risk in public wording
protected-scope boundary clarity
```

Do not submit or request:

