## Boundary rule

The public repository defines the certification category and public-safe proof requirements for consequence-bearing AI execution.

It does not publish the full runtime, private certification process, protected enforcement logic, customer reports, or production deployment architecture.

## Operating principle

Reveal enough to establish the certification standard.

Do not reveal enough to recreate protected runtime enforcement or private certification methodology.
